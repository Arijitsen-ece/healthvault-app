![Healthcare Banner](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1584982751601-97dcc096659c?q=80&w=1200)

---

## 🚨 Problem Statement

**Why can’t patients access digital copies of medical records?**

Healthcare consumers visiting multiple doctors and specialists are forced to carry **physical folders of reports, prescriptions, and X-rays**, because most hospitals do not provide **portable, patient-owned digital records**.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

**HealthVault** is a **secure, patient-owned digital medical record system** that allows users to:

- 📁 Store prescriptions, reports, and scans in one place
- 🔐 Maintain full ownership and privacy of their data
- ⚡ Share records instantly with doctors
- 🆘 Provide emergency access via QR code

👉 No more paper files. No more lost records. No dependency on hospitals.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🔐 Secure & Private

- Firebase Authentication
- Firestore security rules (owner-controlled data)
- Token-based sharing system

### 📂 Smart Record Management

- Upload medical files (PDF / Image)
- Auto-categorization (Prescription / Reports / Scans)
- Timeline-based viewing

### 🚑 Emergency Access

- QR-based public emergency profile
- Instant access for first responders
- No login required in emergencies

### 🔗 Secure Sharing

- Generate temporary secure links
- Share records with any doctor instantly
- Revocable access

```

                  → Cloudinary (file storage)
                  → Share Token System
                  → Emergency QR Access
```
